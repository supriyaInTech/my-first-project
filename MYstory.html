The Starry Nights

Luna loved the night sky. She would often sit on her porch,wrapped ina warm blanket, and gaze at the twinkiling stars.

A Special Wish

One evening, she saw a shooting star!Mke a wish, make a wish! her grandmother used to say. Luna closed her eyes tight and wished for an adventure.

The Adventure Begins

Suddenly, the brightest star in the sky shimmered and grew. A soft light envoloved Luna.Your wish is granted, a gental voice wispered.

Luna opened her eyes. The stars seemed closer, brighter, and suddenly, she was floating among them. Her adventure had begun.
