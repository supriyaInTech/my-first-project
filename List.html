Steps to make Spaghetti Carbonara.

  1. Boil Water: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Prep Sauce: In a medium bowl, wist together the eggs and gratted cheese until smooth.
  3. Fry Meat: In a large skillet over medium heat, saute the guanciale or pancetta until crispy and the fat has rendered
  4. Cook Pasta: Add thespaghetti to the boiling water and cook until firm to the bite.
  5. Reserve water: Before draning, scoop out about 1 cup of starchy pasta water.
  6. Combine: Add the hot, drained pasta to the skillet with the meat fat and toss to coat.
  7. Emulsify: Remove the skillet from heat and wait for 30 seconds.Slowly pour in the egg and the cheese mixture while tossing vigorously to creat a creamy sause, adding splashes of pasta water if needed.
  8. Garnish with plenty of cheese and black pepper
