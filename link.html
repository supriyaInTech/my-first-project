Arc Shot

A shot in which the subject is photographed by an encircling or moving camera.

Interlude

A brief,entervening film scene or sequence, not specifically tied to the plot, that appears within a flim.

Prologue

A speech preface, introduction, or brief scene preceding the main action or plot of a flim; contrast to epilogue
